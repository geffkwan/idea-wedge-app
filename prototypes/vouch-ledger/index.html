04How it works

Four events.
One record.

Nothing to install, nothing to learn. "Verify" is a button, not a wallet. Every record is signed, sealed and portable.

[01] CLAIM

Write the line.

Add a resume claim exactly as you would put it on paper. One line, one thing you did.

> claim.create text="Shipped mobile 0→1"
// status: unverified
[02] VOUCH

Someone who was there signs.

A former manager, peer or client proves their identity, confirms the one claim they saw, and signs it onto both records.

> vouch.signed by=manager tier=3
// weight 1/2 → request second vouch
[03] TASK

Get paid. Get attested.

Take paid tasks from startups: test a product, sit for an interview, label data. Payout in escrow, attestation on completion.

> task.completed payout=$120 net=$96
// attestation issued by company
[04] LINK

Paste one link.

A hiring manager opens it and sees who vouched for what. No account, no fee, no sales call.

> record.read viewer=anon cost=$0
// 4 verified · 1 pending
